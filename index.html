From the line to the system

I learned manufacturing on a footwear production floor, following how a work order, its materials and its people really move through a plant. That is still how I start every project: on the shopfloor, with the operators and process engineers.

Since 2022 I have worked at Fujikura Fiber Optics Vietnam with a Japanese engineering team, taking over production software through on-site handover and building new systems for the digital factory initiative. My work covers production data collection, 4M compliance, material traceability and equipment interfacing, backed by SQL Server and integrated with ERP.

I care about software that operators trust and engineers can extend: clear data models, stored procedures that hold up under load, and integrations that keep running shift after shift.
